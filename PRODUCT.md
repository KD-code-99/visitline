# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Agent-proposed under the user's delegated research/build request: buildless HTML/CSS/JS, local Node server, no runtime dependencies or paid services.

## Users
Proposed primary user: a small event host preparing useful access information. Visitor: someone checking a recorded route against their own mobility requirements. No user interviews have been executed.

## Product Purpose
Make the relevant part of the whole visit understandable, especially which recorded segment is incompatible or unconfirmed. Success in this prototype means a correct, legible record-to-visitor-card round trip.

## Positioning
Event-specific host authoring plus three-state dependency evaluation. Detailed guides and personalised routing already exist; first-in-market or superior user outcomes are not claimed.

## Operating Context
Manual observations about a temporary event setup; browser-local work; exported snapshots. All demonstration places and measurements are fictional.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Selected checks only: steps, user-selected clear width, passage availability and observation completeness. Unknown information remains unknown. No physical safety, regulatory compliance or all-needs accessibility verdict. No automatic measurements or personal-profile uploads.

## Evidence on Hand
Primary surveys, official event guidance and competitive comparisons in `docs/idea-evaluation.md`. No deployed app, customer testimonials, field measurements, user study or confirmed submission exists at this point.

## Product Principles
Show the causal reason. Preserve unknowns. Respect the visitor's own requirements. Keep records portable. Attribute observations and snapshot limitations honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The app must work with keyboard input, screen readers and a narrow mobile viewport. Color supplements explicit state text. The limited mobility-focused prototype is not a claim to cover every disability or access need.
